# Build a Content Brief
## Choose the scope
Use a Content Brief for one article/page. Use a Campaign Brief for a coordinated marketing project with channels, budget and timing. Do not require a full campaign plan for every article.
## Process
1. Inherit brand context, research, priorities and sources; do not invent a new audience.
2. Confirm page action, scope, user need and decision stage.
3. Fill the relevant fields below. Record missing factual evidence as a material request, not a product claim.
4. Check that the outline answers the need, the call to action fits the reader's stage, and measurement is practical.
## Content Brief fields
- Business Objective: the business action and the content's role.
- Target Audience / User Need: reader, scenario, problem and decision stage.
- Primary Keyword / Supporting Queries: target expressions and their need group.
- Search Intent / Page Type: primary/secondary intent and appropriate format.
- Content Angle / Value: useful differentiation and supporting evidence.
- Suggested Title / Meta Description: clear, natural wording; no guaranteed display length.
- Outline: headings, questions to answer and evidence needed in each section.
- Evidence: source references, verified product facts, tests/expert input, and gaps.
- Internal Links: verified existing URLs, anchor text and purpose.
- CTA: the next action, placement and verified destination or required destination.
- Voice / Constraints: language, style, terminology and prohibited claims.
- Measurement: available baseline, metric definitions, source and review window.
- Owner / Delivery: suggested responsibility, timing and acceptance criteria.
## Campaign Brief fields
Business Objective; Market and Consumer Insight (evidence versus hypothesis); Target Audience; Positioning and Key Message; Marketing Strategy; Channel and Action Plan; Budget and KPI (currency, period, assumptions); Timeline and Risk Management; Measurement and Next Step.
If no budget is provided, describe resource needs or clearly labelled scenarios, not invented achievable ROI. Connect each content task to the project objective.
