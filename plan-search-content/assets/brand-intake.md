# Brand Intake
Fill from supplied or verifiable information. Unknown is acceptable. Do not make every field a mandatory user question.

| Field | What to provide |
|---|---|
| Brand / Website | Name, homepage and relevant URLs |
| Task | Desired outcome or named capability |
| Business Objective | What should readers do? |
| Market / Language | Country, language, region and relevant time |
| Products / Services | Core offer and verified specifications/advantages |
| Target Audience | People, scenario, needs and decision stage |
| Positioning / Voice | Message, tone and prohibited claims |
| Existing Content | URLs, formats, dates and known performance |
| Research Inputs | Queries, keywords, competitors, interviews and sources |
| Measurement Inputs | GSC/GA4 exports, periods, filters, event definitions and change log |
| Constraints | Time, budget, people, evidence and approvals |
| Success / Review | Metrics, review window and decision conditions |

Minimum input depends on the task: research needs a sufficiently described offer, market and goal (a website can supply much of this); writing needs a brief or audience/goal/facts; diagnostics needs actual metrics and clearly defined periods. Continue independent work while identifying critical gaps.
